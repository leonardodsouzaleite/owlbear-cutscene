# Owlbear Cutscene

Bring cinematic moments to your tabletop sessions. **Owlbear Cutscene** lets the Game Master launch a video that opens over the play area for everyone connected to the room. When the presentation ends, players return to the Owlbear table.

## Features

- **Room-wide playback:** the Game Master sends one start command to connected participants.
- **Full-screen presentation:** the video covers the Owlbear play area while the cutscene is running.
- **Start delay:** give participants time to receive the command and buffer the video.
- **GM controls:** pause, resume, or stop the presentation.
- **Automatic return:** the player window closes when the Game Master ends the cutscene or the video finishes.
- **Video list:** save frequently used video links in the Game Master's local browser library.

## Getting started

1. Add Owlbear Cutscene to your Owlbear Rodeo profile using the **Install extension** link above.
2. Enable the extension in your room.
3. As the Game Master, open the Cutscene action.
4. Enter a title and a direct HTTPS link to a video file, or select a saved item.
5. Choose a preparation delay and select **Start for everyone**.

Players who are connected to the room receive the presentation automatically. They do not need to open the extension panel.

## Video compatibility

Use a direct, publicly accessible HTTPS URL to a video file. MP4 files encoded with H.264 video and AAC audio are recommended. A link to a video-sharing webpage is not the same as a direct media URL.

The extension does not host, upload, transcode, or stream video files. The Game Master is responsible for choosing a media host that allows all players to access the file and for ensuring that they have the right to show it to their group.

## Important notes

- The video list is saved locally in the Game Master's browser; it is not synchronized between devices.
- Playback is scheduled on each participant's device. Network latency and buffering can cause small timing differences.
- Browser and device autoplay policies may require a participant to tap **Play video** before audio/video playback can begin. Test with your group's devices before a session.
- The presentation covers the Owlbear play area, not the browser or operating-system interface. Use the browser's own full-screen mode to hide browser controls.
- This is an independent community extension and is not made by or affiliated with Owlbear Rodeo.
